## Classification Vocabulary

- `In scope`: behavior may be planned as YAC-native requirements after the clean-room gate clears.
- `Deferred`: behavior is not part of the first YAC-native implementation wave or depends on later product/architecture decisions.
- `Spike required`: behavior needs a security, product, architecture, UX, or feasibility spike before any implementation plan may proceed.
- `Excluded`: behavior is intentionally out of scope until a separate explicit approval exists.
- `Feature/config gate`: the future switch or acceptance gate that must keep risky behavior disabled, narrowed, or unavailable by default.
